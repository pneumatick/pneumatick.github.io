About Me

While pursuing a bachelor's degree in Computer Science at the University of Rhode Island, I found that I like the parts of software where data, systems, design, and problem-solving overlap, which is why I've ended up doing a bit of everything: building local tools that solve specific problems like a searchable YouTube playlist archiver, analyzing data and creating visualizations, tinkering with self-hosted AI models, and poking at hardware/software projects just to figure out how things that I would otherwise take for granted work.

My interests sit at the intersection of backend systems, data, and low-level software. I enjoy projects where understanding the underlying system matters as much as getting the immediate problem solved.
